1. What VegaSort does

VegaSort is an on-device Android file-manager that helps you triage new files (photos, videos, screenshots, downloads, WhatsApp/Telegram media) via a floating bubble or notifications, bulk-sort existing files via Sorting Wizard, and optionally auto-move future files via AutoSort rules. Link shares can be triaged to categories. Folders and history browse locally from the on-device database.

Files are organized locally on your device only. The app does not upload your files (see the Privacy Policy).

2. Permissions you grant (why each exists)

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — required. Core file-manager function: bulk triage across Camera, Screenshots, Download, WhatsApp/Telegram media and SD/USB destinations, which per-folder pickers cannot do. Without it sorting cannot run; the first screen blocks entry until granted.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — required at onboarding for Bubble (default). Shows the floating bubble, expanded sorting panel, and links panel. After onboarding you can switch to Notification mode in Live → Interaction Mode; the bubble is unavailable in that mode by design.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13+) — recommended. Keeps the foreground-service notice visible and delivers per-file, grouped, AutoSort, bulk and trash notices. If denied, those surfaces are silent but your files stay intact; you can grant later in the Permissions screen.
  • Skip Battery Optimization (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS) — recommended, never required. Reduces the chance Samsung/Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo kill the background service. The app works without it but may miss detections on aggressive OEM skins.

OS-level grants (delete/trash consent, storage volume mount/eject) are requested in context, one dialog per batch.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Back up important files. Sorting moves (or copies, if you turn off Move original file) and System-Trash actions are performed on your instruction or your AutoSort rules. Verify destinations in the Folders tab before enabling silent rules.
  • Move vs Copy: Move original file defaults ON (original deleted after verified copy). Turn it OFF to keep originals; Wizard and AutoSort copy warnings apply.
  • System Trash: AutoSort trash rules use Android's MediaStore.createTrashRequest (media files only — silent first, system dialog fallback per burst). Trashed items stay listed in History → Filed with a Recover button; unclaimed items age out with the standard 7-day History prune. AutoSort permanent-delete rules and panel Delete run a batched createDeleteRequest and cannot be undone.
  • Removable volumes: ejecting SD/USB marks folders unavailable until re-mounted or re-picked. Do not remove media mid-move.
  • Lawful content: only sort content you own or may manage. Do not use VegaSort to infringe copyright or handle unlawful material.
  • No refreshing of revoked grants by us: if you or the OS revokes All-Files/Overlay/Notifications, affected features pause or fall back (Live → notifications; bubble hidden) until you re-grant in system Settings via Dashboard → Permissions.

4. Service behavior & limits

  • Stopping VegaSort Service pauses Live detection and Instant AutoSort; Daily AutoSort drains, Folders/History/Links-screen browsing, and notification Move/Dismiss actions keep working; Wizard/share restarts the service.
  • Detection is best-effort across OEMs 12–17; Doze, battery-saver, and manufacturer task killers may delay background work. The 15-min reconcile watermark and tracked re-check bound lateness but do not guarantee instant delivery.
  • Retention: pending items prune after 24h, filed/unfiled history after 7 days. Export links you want to keep via the Links screen TSV backup.
  • Ads: Google AdMob banner; consent via the EU popup (UMP) and Ad privacy choices in the Privacy & Terms dialog (onboarding + Settings); the choices row appears only where Google requires it.
