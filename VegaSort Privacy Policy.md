# Privacy Policy for VegaSort

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **VegaSort** handles your data.

## 1. On-Device Processing (No Data Collection)
VegaSort operates **fully on-device**. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, photos, or files to external servers. All operations are processed locally on your device.

## 2. Permissions and Data Usage
To provide its core functionality of local file management and organization, VegaSort requires specific device permissions:
* **All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE):** This permission is used exclusively to scan, read, and organize different file types locally on your device. This data is never uploaded, shared, or used for any other purpose.

## 3. Third-Party Services
VegaSort does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising networks that collect or track your data.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official development channels or email: vegamanaslabs@gmail.com.
