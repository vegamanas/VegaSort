VegaSort

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-29

1. Summary

  • Your files never leave your device for sorting. Detection, moves, history, links, and rules all run locally.
  • The only network use is ads: Google AdMob may collect and share your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions, diagnostic/performance information, and device/account identifiers such as the Advertising ID and app set ID. This is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. EU/UK/CH users see a UMP consent popup first; where Google requires it, the in-app Privacy & Terms dialog shows Ad privacy choices so you can change your choice anytime.
  • No account and no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The AdMob SDK described above is the only third-party network SDK.

The app does not request or access contacts, device location, microphone, camera recording, SMS, or a broad package inventory (a curated, Play-compliant <queries> list only). AdMob may estimate general location from your IP address as described in §5.

4. Retention

  • Pending (unsorted) items: pruned after 24 hours.
  • Filed/unfiled history: pruned after 7 days.
  • Share-relay cache: age-ordered sweep to 500 MB.
  • Uninstalling removes all app-private data. If you enable Android system backup, the on-device database and preferences are included in that backup; such backup content is stored by Google's backup infrastructure.

5. Sharing & third parties

  • No sale of personal data. Files, history, rules and links are never shared.
  • Google AdMob may collect and share your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app interactions (app launch, taps, video views), diagnostic/performance information (launch time, hang rate, energy usage), and device/account identifiers (Advertising ID, app set ID) for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Where Google requires it, personalization can be revoked any time via the in-app Privacy & Terms → Ad privacy choices entry or the OS ad settings; you can also delete/reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads at any time.
  • No other networked SDK is bundled for sorting; there is no server.

6. Data safety

The app's Play Data safety declaration covers the AdMob SDK data described above: approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or other IDs — collected and shared with Google for advertising/marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention; encrypted in transit; deletion requests via the contact below or the Android ad-ID controls. The app itself does not register accounts and does not transmit your files or history anywhere.

7. Your rights & choices

  • Grant / deny / revoke any permission any time in the app's Permissions screen or the Android settings.
  • Switch to notification mode after onboarding in Live → Interaction Mode (no overlay needed); bubble features disable and notifications carry sorting.
  • Access/delete your data inside the app (Folders, History; clearing history; removing folders releases their access grants; link TSV export for portability).
  • EU/UK/CH: UMP consent popup at first use + Ad privacy choices (shown only where Google requires it) to withdraw consent; contact vegamanaslabs@gmail.com for access/erasure/objection requests — we respond within statutory timelines.

8. Changes

Material changes will be announced in-app and via this page; the effective date at the top always shows the current version.
